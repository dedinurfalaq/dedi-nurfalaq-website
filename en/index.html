Professional Summary

A Career Built Where Finance Meets Technology

I am a seasoned banking professional with deep experience in electronic banking, digital channels and business development. As Head of Department at PT Bank Woori Saudara Indonesia 1906, Tbk, a publicly listed bank backed by South Korea's Woori Financial Group, I led the electronic banking platforms and digital payment services that customers relied on every day. I also helped shape the bank's core banking plan after the merger of Bank Woori Indonesia and Bank Saudara, work that required turning complex change into clear plans, aligning business and IT teams, and delivering within a strict regulatory environment.

Before that, I built my commercial foundation in financial technology and responsible business. As Marketing Manager and Business Development Manager at PT Alto Network, I grew partnerships for payment network services. At Indonesia Business Links, I worked with companies on ethical business practice and corporate social responsibility.

Today I serve as Executive Director of Wakafinaja, lead productive waqf strategy and institutional development, teach as a guest lecturer in postgraduate programmes, and advise Universitas Bani Saleh on strategic planning. I hold a Master of Management and a Bachelor of Social Sciences, and I co-authored a peer-reviewed study on corporate performance in an Indonesian foreign-owned bank (DIJEMSS, 2024).

My guiding principle is simple: as long as I live, I choose to be useful.
